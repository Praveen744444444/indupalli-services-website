Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Indupalli Services ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and services (the "Platform"). By using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account informationName, email address, phone number, password
Profile informationLocation, work experience, skills, "about me" summary, profile photo
Resume & application dataUploaded resume files, cover letters, job applications and their status
Recruiter dataCompany name, job postings, applicant reviews, chat messages with candidates
Sign-in dataIf you use Google or Facebook sign-in, basic profile info they share with us (name, email)
Payment dataTransaction confirmation from our payment processor — we do not receive or store your full card/bank details

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account securely.
  • To match candidates with relevant job listings and let recruiters review applicants.
  • To let recruiters and candidates communicate through the Platform's chat feature.
  • To process payments for premium features, where applicable.
  • To send account-related notifications (e.g. application updates).
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of the Platform.

3. Who We Share Information With

We share your information only as necessary to operate the Platform:

  • Recruiters can see the profile and resume of any candidate who applies to their job posting.
  • Candidates can see basic job and company information provided by the recruiter.
  • Service providers — we use Supabase for authentication, database, and cloud storage, and Razorpay for payment processing. These providers process data on our behalf under their own strict security and privacy standards.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase's secure cloud infrastructure. We take robust technical measures (such as authentication requirements and access controls) to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password for your account.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can review and update your profile information at any time from your dashboard.
  • You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or request that it be corrected or deleted, by emailing us.
  • You can delete your uploaded resume or profile photo at any time by replacing or removing it from your profile.
  • Deleting your account will remove your profile from active use; some records (such as past applications) may be retained where necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences (such as light/dark mode). We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

7. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for individuals aged 18 and older who are eligible to work. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at contact@indupalliservices.com.
