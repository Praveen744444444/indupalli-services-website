Recruiter Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
1. Start With the Dashboard Overview

Use Active Open Positions and Total Received Applications to understand your current hiring workload before reviewing candidates.

2. Create a New Job Opening

Enter the Job Title, Company, Location, Salary / CTC, Experience, Closing Date, and Notice Period. Add the complete Job Description and use AI Generate Description when you need help drafting it. Select PUBLISH JOB OPENING only after reviewing all fields.

3. Review Inbound Candidate Applications

Open Inbound Candidates to review applications for your jobs. Use Search, Filter by Job, and Refresh Feed to find the right candidate quickly.

4. Evaluate Candidate Match & Skills

Review the Match Score, Skill Set, Experience, and candidate profile information together. Do not rely on the score alone; verify that the resume and profile support the requirements of the job.

5. Open the Candidate Pitch / Profile

Use the Cover Letter / Pitch view to read the candidate's professional summary, pitch, and match analysis before deciding on next steps.

6. Review the Resume

Open the candidate's resume from the Resume column and compare it with the job description, required experience, and skills before progressing the candidate.

7. Communicate With Candidates

Use the Chat action to communicate with candidates about next steps, interviews, clarifications, or additional information.

8. Manage Active Job Postings

Use Active Job Postings to review your live openings, check closing dates, and use the available actions to keep job information current.

9. Maintain Recruiter Response Standards

Respond to candidate messages and interview-related communication promptly. Keep candidate information, resumes, phone numbers, and email addresses confidential.

10. Recommended Recruiter Workflow

Create and publish the job → review inbound applications → filter candidates → check match score and skills → read the candidate pitch → review the resume → chat with shortlisted candidates → update the hiring process.
