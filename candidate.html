Candidate Dashboard Guide

Use this quick guide to build your profile, upload your resume, use AI tools, and apply for jobs.

Quick Start

1. Complete Your Profile

  • Basic Details: Enter your full name, phone number, current location, and total experience.
  • Location: Select your state and city so recruiters can understand where you are based.
  • Save Profile: Click Save Changes at the top of My Profile after updating your details.

2. Add Your Resume

  • Upload: Go to Master Profile Resume and click Upload File.
  • File Type: Use a PDF, DOC, or DOCX resume within the dashboard's upload limit.
  • Use Everywhere: Your Master Resume can be used for job applications and AI job matching.

3. Build Your AI Profile

  • Add Skills: Enter your core skills such as Java, SQL, Python, Cloud, or Power BI.
  • Generate: Click Generate Profile or Start to create an ATS-ready summary and expanded skills.
  • Review & Save: Check the generated summary, keep the details you want, and click Save Changes.

4. Find & Apply for Jobs

  • Job Discovery: Search by role, company, skill, and job type to find suitable openings.
  • Match Score: Review the Resume AI Matching Score when available before applying.
  • Apply Once: Open the job, review the details, complete the application, and submit it. The button will show Submitted after a successful application.

5. Track Your Applications

  • My Applications: Check every submitted application from the Applications section.
  • Status: Use Refresh Status to see the latest application status.
  • Messages: Use the Chat button to communicate with the recruiter when a conversation is available.

6. Keep Your Profile Strong

  • Update Regularly: Keep your skills, experience, summary, and resume current.
  • Use AI Carefully: Review AI-generated content before saving it to your profile.
  • Apply Smart: Focus on jobs that match your skills and experience instead of applying to everything.
